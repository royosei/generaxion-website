About Us

Our Vision

To see a global network of spiritually awakened believers who live by faith in Jesus Christ, walk in holiness, and operate as influential ambassadors of the Kingdom of God across every sphere of society

Our Mission

To know Jesus Christ deeply in the Holy Spirit and make Him known everywhere by preaching the gospel of rebirth, discipling believers into maturity, and raising purpose-driven individuals who influence society through kingdom principles.We are committed to helping people discover their God-given purpose, develop their spiritual and personal capacity, and deploy their lives to fulfill divine destiny.

Our Mandate

• Preach the rebirth message of Jesus Christ

• Raise Spirit-filled disciples

• Build a network army of believers in the Holy Spirit

• Restore the spirit of authentic apostolic Christianity

• Advance the Kingdom of God across every sphere of society.

Our Core Beliefs

We believe:

• The Bible is the inspired Word of God.

• Jesus Christ is Lord and Savior.

• Salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ alone.

• The Holy Spirit empowers believers for victorious living.

• The Church is the Body of Christ called to influence the world.
